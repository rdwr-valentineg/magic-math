EXTRACTION NOTES - princess (magic-math-princess-assets)
=====================================================
Source: princes.png (single 1536x1024 sheet). Character identified automatically: PRINCESS.
Method: assets were cropped from the supplied sheet only - nothing was redrawn, regenerated or
reinterpreted. Transparent assets were cut with a background-model + alpha-matting pass (edge pixels
re-estimated to remove the sheet's white halo). Interior artwork is unchanged (mean colour difference
vs source on opaque pixels < 0.3 / 255). All files are lossless WebP. No text or elements were added.

FILES EXPORTED PER CATEGORY
  character/    5  (4 poses + select.webp fallback)
  backgrounds/  4
  environment/  5
  decorations/  12
  icons/        7
  TOTAL         33 WebP files + character.json + this file

RESOLUTION WARNING (important)
  The source sheet is only 1536x1024, so every asset is small: characters ~250 px tall, icons 60-130 px,
  backgrounds 268-417 px wide. Nothing was upscaled (per brief). If the game needs sharper art, the sheet
  should be regenerated at a higher resolution and this same extraction repeated.

SELECT.WEBP - SOURCE / FALLBACK
  FALLBACK USED: select.webp is an exact copy of idle.webp.
  Reason: the character in the HOME / CHARACTER SELECT scene is clipped by the panel edge (dress and hair
  run off the frame) and her hair blends into the painted scene, so she cannot be isolated without
  inventing missing artwork.

CHARACTER STATES
  idle / happy / try-again / celebration (and select) share ONE canvas: 245x298 px.
  - Scale left at native source scale (tiara gem is 12-13 px wide in all four poses = consistent).
  - Feet baseline is on the same row (y=279) in every state; skirt is horizontally centred (x=122).
    Suggested pivot: bottom-centre of the feet, i.e. (122, 279) - switching states will not make her jump.
  - Because of the shared canvas, the character files are NOT tightly trimmed (happy/celebration arms,
    wand and stars set the canvas width). Safe padding is ~4%.
  - celebration.webp keeps its stars, sparkles and confetti as part of the pose (they are particles
    belonging to that state, not separable without cutting artwork).
  - Soft ground shadows under the feet are kept as semi-transparent lavender.
  - Enclosed white gaps between hair curls were made transparent (they are background showing through).
    A few small soft-white hair highlights remain and are treated as artwork.

BACKGROUNDS
  Cropped 5 px inside each panel to drop rounded corners / borders (no headings or borders remain).
  Opaque RGB, no transparent holes. Sizes: game 390x414, celebration 347x414, try-again 268x414,
  distant 417x193 (different aspect ratios - the sheet panels are not the same shape).
  HOME / CHARACTER SELECT scene deliberately NOT used as a background.

ENVIRONMENT (5)
  balustrade, rose-arch, flower-urn (urn on pedestal), banner, pillar. Gaps between balusters are
  transparent. Soft ground shadows kept where present (semi-transparent).

DECORATIONS (12)
  tiara, butterfly, ribbon, magic-wand, rose (rose + its 3 leaves as one piece), leaf,
  petal-01..petal-04, heart-petals, leaf-sprig.
  heart-petals and leaf-sprig come from the ENVIRONMENT strip (small loose decorative pieces).

ICONS (7)
  progress-star, progress-heart, progress-gem, bonus-crown, bonus-medal, bonus-bow, bonus-laurel-wreath.

NOT EXTRACTED (and why)
  - Section 01 HOME / CHARACTER SELECT scene as a background (per brief; global Welcome background).
  - Section 06 UI ELEMENTS (GLOBAL): panels, buttons, plus/minus/multiply/divide, home/sound/settings/close,
    and its star/heart/gem progress icons (generic UI, per brief).
  - Section 07 gold star and gem heart: visually identical to icons/progress-star and icons/progress-heart
    (icon versions are slightly larger), so no duplicate files were created.
  - Loose ground petals scattered around the environment objects, plus the petal pair and small petal on the
    right of the environment strip: same look as petal-01..04, skipped as duplicates/scatter.
  - rainbow, sparkle, confetti, flame, crystal: these do not exist as separate objects on this sheet
    (the rainbow only appears inside scene paintings), so no files were made for them.
  - Decorative UI panels: not extracted (not character-themed reusable assets).

QUESTIONABLE ASSETS / KNOWN LIMITATIONS
  - petal-01 and petal-04 look very similar (only rotation/curl differ); kept as variants.
  - The source has soft baked-in glow/shadow around most objects. Where recognisable as a shadow it was
    converted to semi-transparent lavender; a very faint pale rim may remain on a few gold/pink pieces
    (visible only on dark backgrounds): tiara, magic-wand, ribbon, heart-petals.
  - leaf-sprig has a small white highlight where the stems meet (present in the source).
  - No separate drop shadows were kept for floating decorations/icons (they had only a faint glow).
