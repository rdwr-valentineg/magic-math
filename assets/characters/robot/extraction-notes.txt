EXTRACTION NOTES - robot (ROBOT) - magic-math-robot-assets
============================================================
Source: robot.png (single 1536x1024 sheet). Character identified automatically: ROBOT.
Method: every asset was cropped from the supplied sheet only - nothing was redrawn, regenerated or reinterpreted. Transparent assets were cut with a
background-model + alpha-matting pass (edge pixels re-estimated to remove the sheet's white halo). Opaque backgrounds are plain crops. All files are lossless WebP.
No text or elements were added.

FILES EXPORTED PER CATEGORY
  character/    5  (4 poses + select.webp fallback)
  backgrounds/  4
  environment/  9
  decorations/  4
  icons/        6
  TOTAL         28 WebP files + character.json + this file

RESOLUTION WARNING (important)
  The source sheet is only 1536x1024, so every asset is small (characters ~200 px tall, icons 35-130 px, backgrounds ~270-470 px wide).
  Nothing was upscaled (per brief). For sharper art, regenerate the sheet at a higher resolution and repeat the extraction.

SELECT.WEBP - SOURCE / FALLBACK
  FALLBACK USED: select.webp is an exact copy of idle.webp.
  Reason: in the HOME / CHARACTER SELECT scene the character is clipped by the panel edge and overlaps the painted scene, so it cannot be isolated without inventing missing artwork.

CHARACTER STATES
  idle / happy / try-again / celebration (and select) share ONE canvas: 211x237 px, each pose centred horizontally on its body, native scale, and kept at the
  same vertical position the sheet draws them (so jump/celebration poses stay airborne relative to the ground line). No pose was rescaled.
  Body size per pose (width x height, px): idle 135x193, happy 147x204, try-again 131x189, celebration 149x197.
  Particles/confetti/sparkles/speech marks that belong to a pose are kept inside that pose's file.
  Soft ground shadows are kept as semi-transparent tint where they could be separated from the artwork.

BACKGROUNDS
  Cropped 5 px inside each panel to drop rounded corners/borders; no headings or borders remain; opaque, no transparent holes.
  game 348x421, celebration 341x421, try-again 331x421, distant 435x210 (panels differ in aspect ratio).
  The HOME / CHARACTER SELECT scene was deliberately NOT used as a background.
  !! QUESTIONABLE: backgrounds/celebration.webp and backgrounds/try-again.webp have the CHARACTER PAINTED INTO THE SCENE (plus a Hebrew sign/text such as 'כל הכבוד!' with stars).
     Exported exactly as they appear on the sheet, as requested, but they cannot be used as an empty background under the character sprite without the character showing twice.
     They cannot be cleaned without redrawing, which the brief forbids. Consider regenerating these two scenes without the character.
  !! backgrounds/game.webp also contains Hebrew signposts ('צעדים קטנים / להרפתקאות / גדולות' style text) painted into the scene.

DUPLICATES SKIPPED (same artwork appears in two sections; one copy kept)
  deco gear, play arrow, lightbulb, robot head and the two stars (same artwork as the icons section, icon versions kept)

GENERIC APP CONTROLS SKIPPED
  blue play triangle and blue gear/settings icon (generic controls). The whole global UI section 06 (panels, buttons, stars, hearts, gems, + - x /, home, sound, settings, close) was not extracted.

NOT EXTRACTED / DROPPED
  scattered cloud bases under the waterfall rocks; tiny leftover fragments.
  Decorative UI panels were not extracted. Objects that do not exist as separate elements on this sheet have no file.

QUESTIONABLE ASSETS / KNOWN LIMITATIONS
  - environment pieces (arch, circular-platform, rock-outcrop, rock-pile, waterfall, signpost, planter-pillar) overlap in the source and were cut apart along their narrowest connections; expect rough edges and a few plant/rock fragments that belong to a neighbour.
  - environment/rock-outcrop.webp and rock-pile.webp are small, partial pieces.
  - icons/bonus-robot-head.webp merges the head and the separate antenna tip (they are separate blobs in the sheet).
  - The source has a soft baked-in glow/shadow around most objects. Where recognisable as shadow it became semi-transparent tint; a very faint pale rim may remain on some pieces (visible only on dark backgrounds).
  - Environment/decoration pieces that touch in the sheet were separated automatically with seed points; check edges before use.

OTHER
  Several character sheets were supplied together; this package contains only the ROBOT sheet.
